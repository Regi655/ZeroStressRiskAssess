Method

Risk = Impact × Likelihood, each from 1 to 5. Levels: CRITICAL 15–25 HIGH 10–14 MEDIUM 5–9 LOW 1–4.

ImpactMeaning
5 Totalfull control of the machine (root) or of all its data
4 Severeroot reachable by a local user, credential theft, control of an important service
3 Significantaccess to data or to a non-critical service, tampering with application data
2 Limitedinformation useful for an attack, or a weakened defence
1 Minimalbest practice not followed, no direct damage
LikelihoodMeaning
5 Almost certainexploitable by anyone who reaches the machine over the network, without credentials, with common tools; or already happening
4 Likelyexploitable from the network with little effort (guessable password, known technique)
3 Possiblerequires any account on the machine
2 Unlikelyrequires special conditions: traffic interception, physical access, a second flaw
1 Rarerequires being an administrator already, or is theoretical
  • Likelihood starts from how the weakness is exploited and is adjusted with what the tool measured: a network weakness on a port that nmap cannot reach drops by 2; a CVE in the CISA KEV catalogue rises to 5.
  • Findings describing the same weakness form a single problem, with the others as evidence.
  • “(estimate)”: no specific rule, values derived from the finding severity.
